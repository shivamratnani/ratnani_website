When you are placing somewhere between 500,000 and a million AI voice calls a month, the
difference between a 12% and a 20% answer rate is not a rounding error. It is the entire
economics of the channel.

The instinct is to treat this as a procurement problem — find the vendor with the best
attestation story, sign, move on. That instinct is wrong, and it costs you a quarter before
you find out.

## Attestation is necessary and not sufficient

STIR/SHAKEN attestation tells the terminating carrier that someone vouched for your right
to use the calling number. A-level attestation means your originating carrier knows you and
knows the number is yours. That is table stakes — without it you are marked as suspicious
before anything else is evaluated.

What it does not do is tell the analytics engines sitting in front of the subscriber whether
your traffic *behaves* like traffic worth connecting. Those engines — the ones actually
deciding whether your call shows up as "Scam Likely" — are reading call patterns:

- Short-duration call ratio
- Calls per number per day
- Answer-to-attempt ratio over a rolling window
- Complaint rate, weighted heavily

You can hold perfect attestation and still get labeled, because labeling is a behavioral
verdict, not a credential check.

## The architecture that follows from that

Once you accept that reputation is behavioral, the infrastructure question changes. You stop
asking "which vendor" and start asking "how do I shape traffic per number."

That means the routing layer needs to know things the dialer does not:

```
number pool  →  per-number rolling counters  →  routing decision
                (attempts, answers, duration)
```

A number that has been burning through attempts with no answers should be rested, not pushed
harder. A number with a healthy answer ratio can carry more. This is a control loop, and it
belongs in the SIP layer where you can actually see the disposition of every call.

We run this with Jambonz in front of the carrier trunks and HAProxy distributing across
media servers, which gives one place to observe every call leg and one place to make the
routing decision. The alternative — letting the dialer pick numbers round-robin and hoping —
is what most teams do, and it is why most teams plateau.

## What I would tell someone starting

Instrument disposition before you optimize anything. You cannot shape traffic you cannot see.
Get per-number answer rates into a dashboard, watch them for two weeks, and the pattern that
is costing you money will be obvious well before any vendor conversation.
